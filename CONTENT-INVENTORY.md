## 4. Study Abroad — `/study-abroad`

*"Study abroad with confidence — End-to-end guidance, from choosing the right program to visas
and pre-departure."*

**Audience:** high-school graduates (Foundation/Bachelor's) · university students and
professionals (Master's/short courses) · creative applicants needing portfolio coaching.

**7-step process:** Free Consultation → Program Shortlist (3–5 options) → Language & Portfolio
Prep → Documents (CV, SOP, references, translations) → Applications & scholarship tracking →
Offer & Visa (deposit, CAS/LOA, biometrics) → Pre-Departure (housing, insurance, arrival).

Advice to start **6–9 months early**. Free application handling for selected partner universities.
Document checklist, FAQ (work rights, housing), programs (Bachelor's / Master's / Foundation /
Short Programs) and an interactive **2026 QS World University Rankings map** at
`/university-rankings-map`. Consultation signup at `/study-abroad-consultation`.

   an art section the root page lacks.

   (a homepage draft still containing Wix placeholder text: *"This is the space to introduce

   California $5,300 vs $4,500; Brunel £3,800 vs £2,950; Hertfordshire £3,600 vs £2,400;
   Ellesmere £3,600 vs £2,350; New York $4,650 vs $4,300 (meta description).

   `/summer-school-miami-barry` describes the CSUN/Northridge Los Angeles campus, not Barry/Miami.
   `/drawing-13-18` has the title/meta of the 9–12 course.
